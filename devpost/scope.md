# Every Hand
A workshop planning studio that makes a complete hands-on opportunity visible for every learner.

## The Unique Kernel
A deterministic individual-turn scheduler exposes the difference between attending a station and having enough time to use it. It names unserved learners, proves simple resource shortfalls, and compares actual ways to preserve those full turns: extra session time or extra places. The same comparison repairs remaining slots after a simulated resource loss while preserving completed and unaffected active work.

## Who It's For
A teacher or facilitator planning a short practical session with scarce equipment. They currently divide learners into groups and manually estimate whether everyone gets a turn.

## The Core Loop
Inspect the fictional microscope lesson, confirm learners and timing, see individual opportunities and shortage, compare keeping the equipment versus keeping the finish time, inspect named learner before/after paths, apply a chosen schedule, simulate a loss and compare recovery choices, and export a facilitator run sheet.

## Inspiration & Identity
Warm editorial workshop studio: cream paper, forest ink, coral accent, sage station surfaces, expressive serif headings and precise sans-serif utility. A bespoke vector room makes abstract resource capacity tangible.

## Why This Matters to the Learner
The user selected this project and requested meticulous planning, an excellent interface and a working tested proof of concept. Further personal motivation and learning outcomes are not established. Routine product and technical choices were explicitly delegated; this draft records implementation recommendations, not an invented interview.

## What "Working" Looks Like
28 fictional learners each need four minutes on a microscope during a 30-minute session. Three microscopes can schedule 21 full turns; seven learners lack a turn. Keeping three microscopes needs 40 minutes; adding a fourth serves all 28 inside the original session. After a four-to-three loss at minute eight, a 36-minute total session restores all 28 while preserving eight completed turns. Losing one mid-session preserves complete past turns and shows a valid remaining schedule. Printable cards show real individual slots.

## The POC Boundary
One local browser; 1–60 anonymous or fictional learners; a 5–120-minute session; 1–4 ordered tasks with duration and parallel capacity; deterministic slots; inspectable shortage; previewable time/equipment alternatives with fixed full-turn lengths and named consequences; manual edits; optional AI-assisted paste import with review; playback; single-resource-loss repair; real print and file export.

## Later
Teacher field testing, richer dependencies, shared sessions, availability windows, measured transition durations.

## Explicitly Cut
Accounts and multi-user sync add infrastructure without proving individual opportunity. Behavior prediction and learning guarantees exceed scheduling evidence. Automatic actual attendance tracking requires classroom integration. Deployment is deferred; local demo is sufficient for this build.
