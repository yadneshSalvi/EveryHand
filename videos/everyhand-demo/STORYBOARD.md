# Every Hand — five-act demonstration

This video tells teachers and facilitators that a missing individual turn can become a concrete plan, even after equipment is lost.

## Video direction

The five acts are stable editorial units, with planned starts at 0, 30, 62, 96, and 132 seconds and an end at 165 seconds. Use actual TTS durations and Deepgram word timestamps to align reveals; preserve natural speech and add readable holds rather than forcing a fast continuous read. The time-coded shots below are targets to refine against those timestamps.

Use actual app captures as the visual source of truth. Camera moves magnify evidence already present in the capture; never reconstruct a result, count, source rectangle, print row, or control. Avoid zooming a low-resolution capture beyond its useful detail. End each move with a readable hold. Show click targets immediately before their state change. Keep persistent native context where needed to distinguish baseline, preview, applied, simulated loss, or a separate experiment.

Use the app's own palette and type, restrained original code-generated music, and sparse action sounds. Narration stays dominant. No stock classroom footage, testimonial, dramatic warning montage, unrelated logos, code tour, or simulated deployment. Separate SRT captions are required; any baked-in captions must leave functional evidence readable.

This storyboard is an executable capture plan, not a claim that these shots already exist. Capture filenames are intentionally unassigned; the capture owner records real files and verified source times. Asset candidates are the actual product states named below, never invented screenshots.
